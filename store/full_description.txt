Find hot springs across the United States on one fast, simple map. Hot Springs Map: Wildflower Touring shows about 6,000 thermal springs, colored by water temperature from cool through near boiling, so you can plan a soak, a road trip, or a geology outing.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Browse springs on topo, satellite, and street basemaps
• Filter by state, minimum temperature, land manager, and soaking estimate
• See known soaking spots at a glance: a house icon for commercial ones (resorts, pools, bathhouses) and a hot-spring icon for wild ones
• Tap a spring for its temperature, location, land manager, description, and photos, with a one-tap link to directions
• Sort by distance from you (your location stays on your device)
• Save springs, mark the ones you have visited, and save map areas for offline use

The soaking estimate is the app's own judgement from public information. Always check access, safety, and current rules before you go: many springs are on private land, some are closed, and some are hot enough to scald.
