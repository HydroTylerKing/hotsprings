About the Hot Springs Map

This map brings back the hot springs viewer NOAA retired in 2025. It shows all 1,661 thermal springs in the Thermal Springs List for the United States, compiled by Berry, Grim and Ikelman for NOAA in 1980. Use it to find a warm soak near your next trip, see which springs sit on public land, and jump straight to directions.

What you can do

  • Browse springs on topo, satellite, street or shaded-relief maps.
  • Filter by name, state, minimum temperature and land ownership, or sort by distance from you.
  • Tap a spring for its temperature, location, land manager, photos and a description, with a one-tap link to Google Maps.

Where the information comes from

  • Springs: NOAA Key to Geophysical Records Doc. No. 12 (1980), archived at doi:10.25921/c8p0-zs06.
  • Land ownership: USGS Protected Areas Database (PAD-US 4.1).
  • Descriptions & photos: Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and Google Maps contributors, credited on each spring.
  • Maps: USGS, Esri, OpenTopoMap and OpenStreetMap contributors.

Get the app

Install this map on your phone for a home-screen icon, full-screen view and offline use. iPhone/iPad: open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android/Chrome: tap Install app below or in the browser menu.

The list hasn't been updated since 1980 and its locations can be off. Many springs are on private or closed land, so check access and conditions before you go, and leave no trace.
